# Explore your family tree

Open your website and enter the shared viewing password. The saved starting
person appears at the top of the descendant tree. Select a person to open their
details, including their birth date and previous generations.

## Move around the diagram

Drag empty space with the left mouse button to pan. Cards remain clickable.
On a phone, scroll with one finger; pinch with two fingers to zoom. A trackpad
pinch or Ctrl+wheel also zooms around the gesture position.

- **+ / −** changes the diagram size.
- The percentage button restores **100%**.
- **Tout voir** fits the whole visible diagram.
- **Recentrer** brings the first couple back into view while keeping your zoom.
- The small **−/+** button between partners hides or shows their descendants.
- **Tout déplier** opens every collapsed branch.

On phones, select the settings heading to close or reopen the controls. Very
short screens start with these controls closed to leave more room for the tree.

## Use the compact mobile view

Select **Vue compacte**, the branching-list icon beside the mobile settings
heading. Children appear beneath each partnership without horizontal panning.
Use **Vue arbre** to return to the diagram. Both views keep your branch-collapse
choices, and switching views preserves their separate scroll positions.

## Find people

Search accepts names with or without accents and ignores case and extra spaces.
It includes birth surnames and current married surnames. Matching names are
highlighted, and ancestors remain visible to show the connections.

To find several people together, separate searches with semicolons, for example
`Camille; Julien`. Anyone matching either name is counted once. Empty searches
are ignored. Select **?** beside the search field for help; it also opens on
hover or keyboard focus.

Searching temporarily opens matching branches. Clear the search to restore your
previous collapse choices. The result count includes matches, not the ancestors
shown for context. A card can still have a matching outline when a display
setting hides the surname that matched.

## Names, dates and keyboard navigation

Use the display options to show surnames and birth years, or to show only birth
surnames. Dates retain the available precision: a year, a month and year, or a
complete date. An unknown date appears as **?**.

When a person card has keyboard focus, use the arrow keys to move between nearby
cards, **Home** to return to the starting person, **End** to reach the last visible
card, and **Enter** or **Space** to open details. Tab navigation and normal
keyboard scrolling are also available.

To enter or correct information, use the separate [owner editor](family-editor.md).
